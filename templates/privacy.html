Privacy Policy

How Spendly collects, stores, and uses your information — written plainly so you know what happens to your data.

Last updated · August 15, 2026

01

Data We Collect

Spendly collects only what we need to run your account and track your expenses. That typically includes:

  • Account details such as your name, email address, and password hash
  • Expense records you enter — amount, category, date, and description
  • Basic usage data such as when you signed in, so we can keep the service secure

We do not ask for bank passwords, card numbers, or other payment credentials.

02

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to operate Spendly and improve the experience. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Create and authenticate your account
  • Show your expenses, categories, and summaries
  • Keep the service reliable and prevent abuse
  • Send important notices about your account or changes to this policy

We do not sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it to advertise third-party products.

03

Data Storage

Your expense data is stored on our servers and is tied to your account. We take reasonable steps to protect it, including hashed passwords and access limited to what the app needs to function.

You remain the owner of the records you enter. If you delete your account, we will remove or anonymize associated personal data, except where we must keep a limited record to meet legal obligations.

04

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of third-party services to host the app, send email, or measure reliability. Those providers only receive the information required to perform that job.

We do not share your expense history with advertisers or data brokers. If we add a new provider that materially changes how your data is handled, we will update this policy.

05

Your Rights

You can review and update the information in your account at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated expense records

To make a request, use the contact details below.

06

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Spendly handles your data, please contact us through the support channels provided in the application.
